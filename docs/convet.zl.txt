================================================================
  ZPP LIBRARY: convert.zl
  Unit Conversion Utilities
================================================================

Import with:
  #import["convert.zl"];

Provides functions to convert between common units of
temperature, distance, weight, speed, and digital storage size.

----------------------------------------------------------------
  TEMPERATURE
----------------------------------------------------------------

cToF(celsius)
  Converts Celsius to Fahrenheit.

fToC(fahrenheit)
  Converts Fahrenheit to Celsius.

cToK(celsius)
  Converts Celsius to Kelvin.

kToC(kelvin)
  Converts Kelvin to Celsius.

----------------------------------------------------------------
  DISTANCE
----------------------------------------------------------------

kmToMiles(km)
  Converts kilometres to miles.

milesToKm(miles)
  Converts miles to kilometres.

mToFt(metres)
  Converts metres to feet.

ftToM(feet)
  Converts feet to metres.

mToInches(metres)
  Converts metres to inches.

cmToInches(centimetres)
  Converts centimetres to inches.

inchesToCm(inches)
  Converts inches to centimetres.

----------------------------------------------------------------
  WEIGHT
----------------------------------------------------------------

kgToLbs(kg)
  Converts kilograms to pounds.

lbsToKg(pounds)
  Converts pounds to kilograms.

gToOz(grams)
  Converts grams to ounces.

ozToG(ounces)
  Converts ounces to grams.

----------------------------------------------------------------
  SPEED
----------------------------------------------------------------

kmhToMph(kmh)
  Converts kilometres per hour to miles per hour.

mphToKmh(mph)
  Converts miles per hour to kilometres per hour.

msToKmh(ms)
  Converts metres per second to kilometres per hour.

kmhToMs(kmh)
  Converts kilometres per hour to metres per second.

----------------------------------------------------------------
  DIGITAL STORAGE SIZE
  (Uses base-1024: 1 KB = 1024 bytes)
----------------------------------------------------------------

bytesToKB(bytes)
  Converts bytes to kilobytes.

bytesToMB(bytes)
  Converts bytes to megabytes.

bytesToGB(bytes)
  Converts bytes to gigabytes.

kbToBytes(kilobytes)
  Converts kilobytes to bytes.

mbToBytes(megabytes)
  Converts megabytes to bytes.

gbToBytes(gigabytes)
  Converts gigabytes to bytes.

formatBytes(bytes)
  Converts a byte count into a human-readable string with an
  automatically chosen unit suffix.
  Examples:
    formatBytes(500)          → "500 B"
    formatBytes(2048)         → "2.00 KB"
    formatBytes(1572864)      → "1.50 MB"
    formatBytes(2147483648)   → "2.00 GB"
